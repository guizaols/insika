Source & scope

Three synthetic category records created for this example. No store, customer, or transaction system was queried.

What this report cannot tell us

No prior period, order count, costs, or targets are included. Growth, average order value, profitability, and target attainment cannot be inferred.

Calculation notes & data limitations

Calculations. Revenue is units multiplied by the stated unit price. Revenue share is each category’s revenue divided by $864. Revenue per unit is $864 ÷ 232, rounded to $3.72; it is not average order value.

Rounding. Shares are rounded to one decimal place. The unit shares are 55.2% for coffee, 27.6% for tea, and 17.2% for pastries. Bar widths are proportional to revenue, with coffee’s $512 setting the displayed maximum.

Limits. This is a fictional day with fixed prices. Refunds, discounts, taxes, and delivery fees are not modeled. The report date and reporting period are illustrative, not evidence of a live extraction.
